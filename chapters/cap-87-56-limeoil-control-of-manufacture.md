### CHAPTER 87:56

### LIMEOIL (CONTROL OF MANUFACTURE) ACT

*1950 Ed. Ch. 30 No. 19. 14 of 1949. Commencement.*

An Act relating to the manufacture of limeoil.

### [21 ST A PRIL 1949]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Limeoil (Control of Manufacture) Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act—

“manufacturer” means any person superintending or directing the manufacture of limeoil;

“manufactory” means any distillery for the distillation of limeoil and includes any place where limeoil is extracted by manual or other process.

*Licences for manufacture of limeoil.*

**3.** Subject to the provisions contained below, every person who intends to operate a manufactory which before the commencement of this Act was not in operation shall apply to the Minister for a licence to operate it, and the Minister may issue any such licence subject to such conditions as he may think fit, or refuse to grant any such licence without assigning any reason for the refusal or cancel any such licence issued by him. Nothing herein contained shall apply to any grower of limes in respect of manufacture of limeoil therefrom on the estate on which the same are grown.

*Penalty for operating contrary to provisions of Act.*

**4.** Any person who operates a manufactory contrary to any provision of this Act is liable on summary conviction to a fine of three thousand dollars, and all machinery, implements, utensils, materials, and any limes and limeoil found upon the premises where the manufacture has been carried on shall be forfeited.

*Contravention of conditions of licence.*

**5.** Any manufacturer who contravenes the conditions under which any licence is issued under this Act is liable on summary conviction to a fine of one thousand five hundred dollars, and the Minister may suspend or cancel the licence of any such manufacturer.
