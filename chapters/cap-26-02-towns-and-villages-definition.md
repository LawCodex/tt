### THE ATTORNEY GENERAL AND LEGAL AFFAIRS

### TOWNS AND VILLAGES (DEFINITION) ACT

*1950 Ed. Ch. 40 No. 2. 21 of 1919.*

An Act relating to the definition of the expressions “Town” and “Village” as used in Acts.

*Commencement.*

### [25 TH N OVEMBER 1919]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Towns and Villages (Definition) Act.

*Power to define expressions “town” and “village”.*

**2.** Where an Act contains any enactment referring to a town or village, or to any town or any village, but does not define or give power to define the expression “town” or “village” for the purposes of that enactment, the President may, by Order, declare any area to be a town or village, as the case may be, for the purposes of that enactment.

*Power to fix limits of named towns and villages.*

**3.** Where an Act contains any enactment referring to a named town or a named village, but does not fix or give power to fix the limits of that town or village for the purposes of that enactment, the President may, by Order, fix the limits of that town or village, as the case may be, for the purposes of that enactment.

*Construction of “town” and “village” in default of Order.*

**4.** In default of or subject to the terms of any Order made under this Act, any enactment referring to a town or village or any town or any village or to a named town or a named village shall be construed and have effect in the same manner in all respects as if this Act had not been passed.

*Commencement of Order.*

**5.** An Order made under this Act shall not come into operation until the end of one month from the date of the Gazette in which it is published.

*Order subject to negative resolution.*

**6.** An Order made under this Act shall be subject to negative resolution of Parliament.
