### CHAPTER 13:50

### REMISSION OF PENALTIES ACT

*1950 Ed. Ch. 4. No. 24. 9 of 1886. Commencement.*

An Act relating to the remission of penalties.

### [20 TH A UGUST 1886]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Remission of Penalties Act.

*Remission of penalties. [136/1976].*

**2.** The President may remit in whole or in part any sum of money which under any Act may be imposed as a penalty or forfeiture on a convicted offender, although such money may be in whole or in part payable to some party other than the State, and may pardon any person who may be imprisoned for non-payment of any sum of money so imposed, although the same may be in whole or in part payable to some party other than the State.
