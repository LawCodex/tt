### MONGOOSE ACT

*1950 Ed. Ch. 25. No. 6 8 of 1918. Commencement.*

An Act relating to the importation and destruction of mongoose.

### [27 TH A PRIL 1918]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Mongoose Act.

*Mongoose not to be imported.*

**2.** (1) No person shall import or cause to be imported into Trinidad and Tobago any live animal of the description known as mongoose.

(2) Any person who contravenes this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine of one thousand dollars.

*Power of Customs officer.*

**3.** Any officer of Customs finding a live mongoose among any goods or baggage entering Trinidad and Tobago shall forthwith seize the same and destroy or cause the same to be destroyed.

*Mongoose not to be kept.*

**4.** (1) No person shall keep or have in his possession any live mongoose.

(2) Any person who contravenes this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine of one thousand dollars.

(3) The convicting Magistrate shall order any mongoose in respect of which an offence under this section has been committed to be destroyed.

*Search warrant.*

**5.** (1) Any Magistrate or Justice who is satisfied by proof on oath that there is reasonable cause for believing that there is in any house, building, premises or other place any mongoose, may issue a warrant under his hand authorising a police officer named in the warrant to enter and search such house, building, premises or other place for such mongoose.

(2) Every such warrant may be issued and executed on a Sunday.

*Entry on land by certain persons in order to destroy mongoose. Ch. 63:56.*

**6.** (1) It shall be lawful for—

(a) the Chief Technical Officer (Agriculture); or

(b) the Chief Plant Protection Officer or any Plant Protection Officer appointed under the Plant Protection Act; or

(c) any person authorised in writing by the Chief Technical Officer (Agriculture),

to enter with such assistants as may be necessary upon any land and search for, capture and destroy mongoose.

*[51/1980].*

(2) The occupier or, if there is no occupier, the owner of any such land shall pay to the Director of Agriculture the sum of two dollars for every mongoose destroyed under this section.

(3) If a mongoose is destroyed by or under the direction of any person named in subsection (1)(a) and (b), any amount received in respect of such destruction shall be paid to the Comptroller of Accounts, otherwise such amount shall be paid by the Chief Technical Officer (Agriculture), when received, to the person by whom or under whose direction the mongoose is destroyed.

*Order on owner or occupier of land to destroy mongoose. Schedule.*

**7.** (1) The Chief Technical Officer (Agriculture) may issue an order in writing in the form set out in the Schedule to the occupier, or if there be no occupier to the owner, of any land, directing him to take within a definite time from the service upon him of such order to be specified therein, all such measures as may be necessary for the destruction of mongoose on his land.

(2) If there is no occupier on any land, service of any order under this Act may be made by affixing the same in some conspicuous part of the land.

*Non-compliance with order.*

**8.** If any owner or occupier fails to comply with the directions contained in any order, any Officer authorised either generally or in a special case by the Chief Technical Officer (Agriculture) may, with or without assistants, enter upon such lands and take such steps as may be necessary for properly and effectively carrying such order into execution.

*Expenses and charges.*

*Ch. 76:04.*

**9.** All payments directed to be made to the Chief Technical Officer (Agriculture) under section 6, and all expenses incurred by or under the direction of the Chief Technical Officer (Agriculture) in carrying into effect any Order made under section 7 shall be a first charge on the land affected by the order, and payment thereof may be enforced forthwith in the manner provided by the Lands and Buildings Taxes Act.

*Protection of persons acting under this Act.*

**10.** (1) No person authorised under this Act shall be deemed a trespasser by reason of any entry or destruction or action taken or thing done under this Act, or be liable for any damages occasioned by carrying out any of the provisions of this Act, unless the same were occasioned maliciously and without reasonable and probable cause.

(2) Proceedings taken under this Act shall not be rendered void by reason of any informality in any order or notice made or given.

*(Section 7).*
