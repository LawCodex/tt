### CHAPTER 64:40

### LIMES EXPORT LEVY ACT

*Ed. Ch. 32. No. 14. 5 of 1942.*

An Act to provide for the Imposition of a levy on limes 1950 and lime products exported from Trinidad and Tobago for the purpose of furthering the interests of the limes industry.

*Commencement.*

### [7 TH M AY 1942]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Limes Export Levy Act.

*Power to impose levy.*

**2.** The Minister may by Order impose a levy upon limes and lime products exported from Trinidad and Tobago at such rate or rates as he may consider expedient.

*Application of the Customs Act. Ch. 78:01.*

**3.** The provisions of the Customs Act shall have effect as though any levy imposed by virtue of this Act was an export duty leviable by virtue of section 6 of that Act.

*Disposal of proceeds of levy.*

**4.** The Comptroller of Customs and Excise shall deposit all proceeds of any levy imposed by virtue of this Act at the Treasury where they shall be credited by the Comptroller of Accounts to a special account and the Comptroller of Accounts shall pay therefrom such sums as the Minister may from time to time direct towards the expenses of the West Indian Limes Association (Incorporated).

*G.1942. p.1248.*

### LIMES EXPORT LEVY (FIXING OF RATES) ORDER

made under section 2

*Citation.*

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Limes Export Levy (Fixing of Rates) Order.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Order—

“clear, clarified, filtered, racked or settled juice” means the juice of the lime exclusive of the oil thereof;

“pipe” of citrate of lime means 6 hundredweights;

“pipe” of concentrated juice means 108 Imperial gallons;

“top pulp juice” means the entire juice of the lime including the oil thereof.

*Rate of levy.*

*Schedule.*

**3.** A levy at the rates set out in the second column of the Schedule is hereby imposed upon the limes and the lime products exported from Trinidad and Tobago which are respectively set out opposite the said rates in the first column of the said Schedule.

*[Subsidiary]*

Limes Export Levy (Fixing of Rates) Order

SCHEDULE Products Rate of levy Green or fresh limes … 12 cents per 160 lbs. or any part thereof. Pickled limes … … 12 cents per 40 gallons cask, or any part thereof. Distilled oil … … 6 cents per lb. or any part thereof.

Ecuelled oil … … 12 cents per lb. or any part thereof.

Top pulp juice … … 1 cent per gallon, or any part thereof. Clear, clarified, filtered, … 1.5 cents per gallon, or any part thereof. racked or settled juice.

Concentrated juice … 60 cents per pipe where the citric acid contained therein amounts to 64 ounces avoirdupois in each gallon, and in proportion for any difference in the quantity of concentrated juice in the pipe or citric acid in the gallon. Citrate of lime … … 60 cents per pipe where the citric acid contained therein amounts to 64 per centum and in proportion for any difference in the quantity of either citrate of lime or citric acid in the pipe.
