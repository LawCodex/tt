PLANT (EXPORT PROHIBITION) ACT An Act to enable the Minister to prohibit or restrict the 1950 Ed. Ch. 32 No. 7. exportation from Trinidad and Tobago of any living plant 9 of 1938. or any seed or fruit.

**2.** In this Act the expression “plant” means any living tree, Interpretation. shrub, root, herb or grass, or any part thereof respectively, capable of being grown, or any seed or fruit.

### I.C.2. BANANA (EXPORT CONTROL) ORDER

made under section 3

*Citation.*

**1.** This Order may be cited as the I.C.2 Banana (Export Control) Order.

*Prohibition.*

**2.** The exportation from Trinidad and Tobago of the plant known as the I.C.2 Banana, other than the fruit thereof, is hereby prohibited except under written permit obtained from the Chief Technical Officer (Agriculture) who may in his discretion grant or withhold the issue of the permit.

*G. 24.10.42.*

### RUBBER PLANTS AND SEEDS (EXPORT CONTROL) ORDER

made under section 3

*Citation.*

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Rubber Plants and Seeds (Export Control) Order.

*Prohibition.*

**2.** The exportation from Trinidad and Tobago of plants or seeds of Para Rubber (Hevea Brasiliensis) is hereby prohibited except under the authority of and subject to the conditions and restrictions set out in a written permit granted by the Chief Technical Officer (Agriculture) under the provisions of this Order.

*Discretionary power.*

**3.** The Chief Technical Officer (Agriculture) shall have full discretionary power to refuse permits for the exportation from Trinidad and Tobago of plants or seeds of Para Rubber or to grant such permits subject to such conditions and restrictions as he may think necessary or advisable.

*Particulars.*

**4.** Every application for the grant of a permit under the provisions of this Order shall contain the following particulars:
