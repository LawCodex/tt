### SCHOOLS (MEDICAL INSPECTION) ACT

*1950 Ed. Ch. 14 No. 4. 6 of 1928.*

An Act to make provision for the medical inspection of school children.

*Commencement.*

### [7 TH M AY 1928]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Schools (Medical Inspection) Act.

*Interpretation. [45 of 1979].*

**2.** In this Act—

*Ch. 39:01.*

“school” means any school whether public or private within the meaning of the Education Act;

“School Medical Officer” means a member of the Medical Board designated by the Minister for the purposes of this Act.

*Power to enter and inspect.*

**3.** A School Medical Officer or any person appointed by him in writing for such purpose has the right to enter and inspect, with or without notice, any school during school hours.

*Medical inspection of pupils.*

**4.** A School Medical Officer may arrange for the medical inspection of all pupils attending any school.

*Protection of officers.*

**5.** No action shall be brought against any School Medical Officer for anything done by him in good faith and in furtherance of the purposes of this Act.

*Report on inspection.*

**6.** A School Medical Officer may from time to time inspect any school and shall after every such inspection report in the prescribed form to the Chief Medical Officer.

*Principal or headmaster or headmistress to keep records and assist officers. [45 of 1979].*

**7.** The Principal or headmaster or headmistress of every school shall keep such records as may be prescribed and shall give every assistance and facility to any School Medical Officer visiting a school for the purpose of inspection.

*Regulations.*

**8.** (1) The Minister may make Regulations for all or any of the following purposes:

(a) prescribing the intervals at which a pupil attending any school shall be medically inspected;

(b) prescribing the nature of the medical inspection of pupils attending school, and the forms and records to be used in relation to the medical inspection;

(c) generally for the better carrying out of the provisions of this Act.

(2) Regulations made under this section shall be subject to affirmative resolution of Parliament.
