### CHAPTER 57:02

### STATE GRANTS AND LEASES (RE-ISSUE) ACT

*1950 Ed. Ch. 27. No. 6. 20 of 1904.*

An Act relating to grants and leases of State land which have been lost or destroyed.

*Commencement.*

### [17 TH N OVEMBER 1904]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the State Grants and Leases (Re-issue) Act.

*Certificate of loss or destruction.*

**2.** (1) Whenever it appears to the satisfaction of the President that any grant of land alienated in fee from the State before the 9th August 1889, or any lease of State lands, has been destroyed or lost, the President may certify to the issue of such grant or lease.

*Schedule.*

(2) Such certificate shall state the date on which the grant or lease of State lands was issued, the name of the person to whom issued, the description of the parcel of land, and the nature and extent of the estate or interest granted or leased, and may be in the form set out in the Schedule.

(3) Every such certificate shall be signed by the President and shall be under the Public Seal of Trinidad and Tobago and shall be registered in the office of the Registrar General.

(4) Every such certificate shall, when registered, be admissible in evidence in all Courts of Justice, to the same extent and for the same purposes as the original grant or lease would have been admissible.

*Proof of interest.*

**3.** (1) Any person applying for a certificate under section 2 shall, in addition to the proof of the destruction or loss of such grant or lease, furnish proof of his interest therein.

*Survey and advertisement.*

(2) The President may require the lands in respect of which a certificate is applied for to be surveyed, and the application for such certificate to be advertised in the Gazette and in such other newspaper as he may direct, and the cost of such survey and advertisement shall be paid by the applicant.
