### TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING (MISCELLANEOUS LICENCES) ACT

An Act to require applicants for certain licences to satisfy 24 of 1982. licensing authorities that planning permission has been granted.

[7 TH S EPTEMBER 1982] Commencement.

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Town and Country Planning Short title. (Miscellaneous Licences) Act.

**2.** Where under any written law a licence is required to carry Licence not to be granted on any business or other activity on any land or in any building, a unless planning permission is licence may not be granted, notwithstanding that the requirements obtained. of the written law have been met, unless the applicant satifies the licensing authority—

(a) that permission was obtained under the Town Ch. 35:01. and Country Planning Act for the development of that land or the use of that building for the purposes contemplated in the application for the licence; or

(b) that such permission is not required.
