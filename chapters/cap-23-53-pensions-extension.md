### CHAPTER 23:53

### PENSIONS EXTENSION ACT

*34 of 1961.*

An Act to extend the Pensions Act to all monthly paid persons employed in the Public Service of Trinidad and Tobago.

*Commencement.*

### [19 TH O CTOBER 1961]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Pensions Extension Act.

*Interpretation. [12 of 1967]. Ch. 23:57.*

**2.** (1) In this Act “Provident Fund” means the Provident Fund maintained under the Provident Fund Act.

*Schedule.*

(2) A reference in this Act to a statutory board shall be read and construed as a reference to a board, authority, committee or other body specifically named in the Schedule and a reference to an office or a post in relation to a statutory board shall be construed as a reference to an office or a post under any or all of the authorities, committees or other bodies named as aforesaid, as the case may require.

*Application of Pensions Act. [12 of 1967 \*35 of 1967]. Schedule.*

*Ch. 23:52.*

**3.** (1) Notwithstanding any law in force at the commencement of this Act but subject to this Act, the offices set out in the Schedule shall, subject to subsection (2), be deemed to be and to have been pensionable offices within the meaning of the Pensions Act—

(a) on and after 1st January 1959, in the case of an office in existence on 1st January 1959; and

(b) in the case of an office established after 1st January 1959, on and after the date of the establishment of that office,

and the Pensions Act applies to those offices with effect as of that date.

(2) Subsection (1) applies only to such of those offices described therein as are monthly paid offices in the public service or under a statutory board.

\*Deemed to have come into operation on 19th October 1961.

*Service under specified bodies preceding pensionable service. Continuous service. [12 of 1967].*

*Ch. 23:52.*

**4.** (1) A person, other than a depositor to the Provident Fund who has not elected under section 5 to continue to be a depositor to the Provident Fund, who has become the holder of an office that is made a pensionable office by virtue of section 3 and was the holder of such office between 1st January 1959, and the date of the commencement of this Act, is entitled to count towards pension in accordance with the Pensions Act all his service in that office together with any other continuous service immediately preceding such pensionable service under a statutory board, department of Government or other authority or body specified in an Order made in that behalf by the then President.

(2) Where in pursuance of the provisions of any law other than the Pensions Laws, a Superannuation Scheme is established for the benefit of the holders of offices under a statutory board, the period of pensionable service together with any other service which by virtue of subsection (1) they may be entitled to count towards pension shall be deemed to be service for the purpose of any such Scheme, and the Minister responsible for Finance shall cause to be paid out of public funds to the credit of the Scheme an amount equal to that which such person would have contributed to that Scheme had he during the period of his pensionable service made contributions in the manner and at the relevant rates prescribed by the Scheme.

*Ch. 23:52.*

(3) In this section “the Pensions Laws” means the Pensions Act and the Regulations made thereunder and any written laws amending or replacing the same.

*Depositor to Provident Fund may elect to continue to be a depositor.*

**5.** (1) Where a person had been a depositor to the Provident Fund while holding an office that is made a pensionable office by virtue of section 3, if he held that office on 1st January 1959, or at any time since that day and before the commencement of this Act, and on the commencement of this Act is holding that office, he may elect to continue to be a depositor to the Provident Fund and section 3 shall not be applied in respect of his tenure of that office.

(2) An election under subsection (1) shall be by written notice addressed to the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Finance.

*Transfer from Provident Fund to Pensions Act.*

**6.** Where a person employed in the public service at the commencement of this Act had been a depositor to the Provident Fund while holding an office that is made a pensionable office by virtue of section 3, if he held that office on 1st January 1959, or at any time since that day and before the commencement of this Act, and on the commencement of this Act is holding that office and does not elect in accordance with section 5 to continue to be a depositor to the Provident Fund—

*Ch. 23:57.*

(a) he shall forthwith surrender his bonuses from that fund and the interest thereon and all his rights to a gratuity under section 22 of the Provident Fund Act;

*Ch. 23:52.*

(b) he is entitled to count all his service while such a depositor towards pension in accordance with the Pensions Act; and

(c) he is entitled to be repaid his compulsory deposits to the Provident Fund together with the interest thereon to the date of repayment.

*Computation of pension benefits for retired officers.*

**7.** (1) In this section the expression “the amount of the gratuity granted to him under the Provident Fund” does not include the compulsory deposits made by a person under the Provident Fund and the interest thereon.

(2) Where a person who retired from the public service before the commencement of this Act had been a depositor to the Provident Fund while holding an office that is made a pensionable office by virtue of section 3, if he held that office on 1st January 1959, or at any time since that day and before his retirement from the public service, he is eligible—

*Ch. 23:52.*

(a) where the amount of the gratuity granted to him from the Provident Fund is less than the gratuity he would have received on the date of his retirement if he had been retired under the Pensions Act and had elected to take a gratuity and reduced pension thereunder, for a further payment to him of—

(i) a sum representing the difference between the amount of the gratuity granted to him from the Provident Fund and the amount

of the gratuity that he would have received had he on the date of his retirement been retired under the Pensions Act; and

(ii) a pension computed as though on the date of his retirement he had been retired under the Pensions Act and had received Ch. 23:52. the amount of the gratuity granted to him from the Provident Fund together with the further amount under subparagraph (i), if any, as the gratuity receivable by him upon an election under the Pensions Act for a gratuity and reduced pension;

(b) where the amount of the gratuity granted to him from the Provident Fund is equal to the amount of the gratuity he would have received on the date of his retirement if he had been retired under the Pensions Act and had elected to take a gratuity and reduced pension thereunder, for the payment of a pension computed as though on the date of his retirement he had been retired under the Pensions Act and had received the amount of the gratuity granted to him from the Provident Fund as the gratuity receivable by him upon an election under the Pensions Act for a gratuity and reduced pension; or

(c) where the amount of the gratuity granted to him from the Provident Fund exceeds the gratuity he would have received on the date of his retirement if he had been retired under the Pensions Act and had elected to take a gratuity and reduced pension thereunder—

(i) if within one month of the coming into operation of this Act he pays to the Comptroller of Accounts the amount representing the difference between the amount of the gratuity granted to him from the Provident Fund and the amount of the gratuity that he would have received had he on the date of his retirement been retired under the Pensions

*Ch. 23:52.*

Act, for the payment of a pension computed as though on the date of his retirement he had been retired under the Pensions Act and had received the amount of the gratuity granted to him from the Provident Fund less the difference repaid by him, as the gratuity receivable upon an election under the Pensions Act for a gratuity and a reduced pension; or

(ii) if he does not pay to the Comptroller of Accounts the amount referred to under subparagraph (i), for the payment of a pension computed as though on the date of his retirement he had been retired under the Pensions Act and had received the amount of the gratuity granted to him from the Provident Fund less the difference not paid by him as the gratuity receivable upon the election under the Pensions Act for a gratuity and reduced pension but the Comptroller of Accounts shall deduct the amount owed from such pension in equal monthly instalments for any period not exceeding ten years in the discretion of the Comptroller of Accounts.

(3) Subsection (2)(c)(ii) shall not be construed to give any claim or charge upon the estate of a pensioner where a pensioner dies before the total unpaid balance is deducted by the Comptroller of Accounts from the pension of the deceased pensioner.

*Application of Provident Fund Act. Ch. 23:57.*

**8.** After the commencement of this Act the Provident Fund Act applies only in respect of—

(a) daily-paid employees in the service of the Government of Trinidad and Tobago; and

(b) those persons who elect, in accordance with section 5, to continue to be depositors to the Provident Fund.

*Variation of Schedule. [10 of 1971 \*32 of 1972].*

\*9. (1) The President may by Notification vary the Schedule—

(a) by including therein any other statutory board or removing therefrom any statutory board; and

\*Deemed to have come into operation on 4th July 1967.

(b) by adding thereto any other office in the public service or under a statutory board or removing therefrom any such office.

(2) Where a statutory board that has been included in the Schedule establishes a Pension Scheme for the benefit of its officers the statutory board and the offices thereunder shall be removed from the Schedule by the President.

(3) Notwithstanding any other written law, the terms and conditions of service, and any variation thereof, of a person appointed to an office mentioned in the Schedule, shall be subject to the prior approval of the Chief Personnel Officer.

(4) Notwithstanding any other written law, the size of the staff establishment of a Statutory Board, authority, committee or other body mentioned in the Schedule shall not be varied without the prior approval of the Chief Personnel Officer.

*Avoidance of double payment. [20 of 1997]. Ch. 23:59.*

**10.** (1) Notwithstanding the provisions of this Act or any other written law, where, after the coming into force of the Law Reform (Pensions) Act, a Statutory Board, authority, committee or other body is included in the Schedule, a person who is—

(a) appointed to an office mentioned in the Schedule on the establishment of that statutory board, authority, committee or other body; and

*Ch. 88:13.*

(b) entitled to terminal benefits in respect of resignation, retirement, retrenchment or death under a collective agreement, or any other agreement, entered into with that statutory board, authority, committee or other body or in accordance with the Retrenchment and Severance Benefits Act,

*Ch. 23:52.*

shall not be entitled to any benefits under the Pensions Act unless he waives, in writing, his entitlement to all of those terminal benefits.

(2) In subsection (1), “retirement” includes retirement on the grounds of ill health.
