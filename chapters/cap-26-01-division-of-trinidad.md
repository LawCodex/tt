**1.** This Act may be cited as the Division of Trinidad Act.

*Division of Wards into Quarters.*

**12.** The President may by Order divide any Ward into divisions to be known as Quarters, and declare the name by which each such Quarter shall be known.

*Islands not specifically mentioned.*

**13.** Islands not specifically mentioned as being comprised within any County, Ward or Quarter in this Act or in any Order made thereunder shall be deemed for all purposes to be comprised within the County, Ward or Quarter as the case may be, to the sea boundary of which they are nearest measured in a straight line.

*Rivers, etc., as boundaries.*

**14.** (1) Where, in or under this Act, a river, road, trace or crest of a range or spur is made the boundary of any County, Ward or Quarter, the boundary shall, subject to the express provisions of this Act, be deemed to be a line drawn along the middle of the river, road, trace or crest.

(2) The President may by Order direct that boundary posts shall be erected on any crest named in the Order, and thereupon the boundary shall be deemed to be a straight line drawn between such posts.

*Change of names, boundaries, etc., of Wards and Quarters.*

**15.** The President may by Order—

(a) change the name of any Ward or Quarter;

(b) alter the areas comprised within or the boundaries of any Ward or Quarter;

*References to Counties, etc., in Acts, etc.*

**16.** All references in any Act already passed or hereafter to be passed and in every rule, regulation or bye-law made or hereafter to be made and issued under the authority of any such Act, and in any Order in Council, Proclamation or Order made under lawful authority to any County, Ward or Quarter in Trinidad by any name mentioned in this Act or conferred under this Act shall be deemed to be to the particular County, Ward or Quarter as the case may be, defined in this Act or in any Order issued under this Act.
