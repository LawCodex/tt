### CHAPTER 25:06

### CITY OF SAN FERNANDO ACT

*26 of 1988.*

An Act to establish the City of San Fernando and to confer on its Council the powers of the San Fernando Borough Council.

*Commencement. [215/1988].*

### [18 TH N OVEMBER 1988]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the City of San Fernando Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act—

*Ch. 39. No. 7.*

“Borough” means the Borough of San Fernando as referred to in the San Fernando Corporation Ordinance;

“Ordinance” means the San Fernando Corporation Ordinance.

*Borough to be known as City.*

**3.** Upon the commencement of this Act the Borough shall be known as the City of San Fernando, the Borough Council referred to under the Ordinance shall be known as the City Council and the burgesses of the Borough of San Fernando shall be known as the citizens of the City of San Fernando.

*Reference to Borough.*

**4.** A reference in the Ordinance, in any written law or in any instrument or other document to the Borough, the Borough Council and the burgesses shall be construed as a reference to the City of San Fernando, the City Council of San Fernando and the citizens respectively.
