### CHAPTER 67:52

### CONTROL OF IMPORTATION OF LIVE FISH ACT

*1950 Ed. Ch. 32. No. 16. 44 of 1950.*

An Act to control the importation into Trinidad and Tobago of live fish.

*Commencement.*

### [23 RD N OVEMBER 1950]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Control of Importation of Live Fish Act.

*Restriction on importation of live fish.*

**2.** (1) No person shall, without the consent by Notification of the Minister, import into Trinidad and Tobago any live fish other than live fish indigenous to Trinidad and Tobago.

(2) Any person contravening the provisions of this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine of four hundred dollars.

*Power of Customs Officers.*

**3.** Any officer of Customs finding any live fish other than live fish indigenous to Trinidad and Tobago among any goods or baggage entering Trinidad and Tobago shall, if the consent of the Minister for the importation of the same has not been obtained, forthwith seize the live fish and destroy or cause the same to be destroyed.

### MINISTRY OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL AND LEGAL AFFAIRS

### SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION

*GN 57/1954.*

IMPORTATION OF ORNAMENTAL FISH NOTIFICATION made under section 2

Consent has been given for the importation into Trinidad and Tobago of ornamental fish for aquarium use except— the Carp (Cyprinus carpio) and its gold variety (Japanese

“Higoi”);

the Goldfish (Carassius auratus) and all its varieties; the “Prussian Carp” (Carassius carassius);

the “Pirai” (Serrosalmo niger) and allied species;

the “Basses” (Micropterus dolomieu and Micropterus salmoides).
