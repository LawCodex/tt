CHAPTER 64:08 CARONI (1975) LIMITED AND ORANGE GROVE NATIONAL COMPANY LIMITED (DIVESTMENT) ACT An Act to provide for the vesting of the operational undertakings of Caroni (1975) Limited in another company and the vesting of the real estate undertakings of Caroni (1975) Limited and Orange Grove National Company Limited in the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago and to provide for the development and management of certain real estate undertakings.

[1 ST J UNE 2006] W HEREAS Caroni (1975) Limited and Orange Grove National Company Limited are both wholly-owned State Enterprises: And whereas it is expedient to divest and transfer the entire real estate holdings of Caroni (1975) Limited and Orange Grove National Company Limited to the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago:

And whereas Estate Management and Business Development Company Limited, a wholly-owned State Enterprise, has been established for purposes which include the development and management of certain real estate undertakings:

*Short title.*

And whereas Sugar Manufacturing Company Limited, a wholly-owned State Enterprise, has been established to undertake the sugar cane processing and sugar refining business conducted by Caroni (1975) Limited:

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Caroni (1975) Limited and Orange Grove National Company Limited (Divestment) Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act—

*Ch. 81:01.*

“appointed day” means the day on which this Act comes into operation in accordance with section 3;

“Caroni” means Caroni (1975) Limited, a company incorporated in Trinidad and Tobago and continued under the Companies Act;

“Caroni liabilities” means all the liabilities of Caroni to the State and to third parties;

“Company” means Sugar Manufacturing Company Limited, a company incorporated in Trinidad and Tobago under the Companies Act;

“Manager” means Estate Management and Business Development Company Limited, a company incorporated in Trinidad and Tobago under the Companies Act;

“Minister” means the Minister to whom responsibility for the Manager is assigned;

*Second Schedule.*

“operational undertakings” means the property of Caroni other than its real estate holdings, used for the conduct of its sugar cane processing and sugar refining business and more particularly described in, but not limited to that set out in the Second Schedule;

“Orange Grove” means Orange Grove National Company Limited, a company incorporated in Trinidad and Tobago and continued under the Companies Act;

*First Schedule. Commencement. 99/2006. Vesting of real estate undertakings.*

“real estate undertakings” means all the real estate holdings of Caroni and Orange Grove whatsoever and wheresoever situate and more particularly described in, but not limited to those set out in the First Schedule;

“State” means the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago.

\*3. This Act comes into operation on such day as is fixed by the President by Proclamation.

**4.** The real estate undertakings are hereby transferred to and vested in the State to the intent that the State shall succeed to the real estate undertakings, as if in all respects the State were the same person in law as Caroni or Orange Grove, as the case may be. * This Act came into operation on 01.06.2006 — See LN 99/2006.

*Minister may vest operational undertaking.*

**5.** (1) The Minister may, by Order, transfer to or vest in the Company, all or any part of the operational undertakings necessary for the Company to undertake the sugar cane processing and sugar refining business conducted by Caroni and not already leased to or forming part of lands leased to or otherwise vested in the Company.

*Effect of vesting.*

(2) The transfer and vesting under subsection (1), may be subject to such conditions as the Minister may determine.

**6.** (1) Subject to subsection 2 the effect of the vesting of the real estate undertakings and the operational undertakings shall be that from the appointed day—

(a) every contract relating to the real estate undertakings or the operational undertakings to which Caroni or Orange Grove was a party, and existing before the appointed day, except a contract with the State, whether in writing or not, shall be construed and have effect as if the State or the Company, as the case may be, is a party instead of Caroni or Orange Grove, as the case may be; and

(b) for any reference to Caroni or Orange Grove as the case may be, whether expressed or implied, there shall be substituted as respect anything failing to be done on or after the appointed day, a reference to the State.

(2) No transfer or vesting effected by virtue of this Act shall—

(a) operate as a breach of covenant or conditions against alienation;

(b) give rise to any forfeiture;

(c) invalidate or discharge a contract or security relating to the real estate undertakings, the operational undertakings or the Caroni liabilities; or

(d) operate so as to merge any leasehold interest in the reversion expectant in it or so as to invalidate any leases or tenancies.

*Act is a good root of title.*

*Minister may amend First Schedule. Stamp Duty Act, Ch. 76:01 not to apply.*

**7.** As from the appointed day, this Act shall be deemed to be a good root of title to the real estate undertakings, so that any lease granted in furtherance of this Act, shall be conclusive evidence that the person named in such lease is seized of or possessed of or entitled to such land for the estate or interest therein specified.

**8.** The Minister may, by Order, amend the First Schedule.

*Functions of the Manager. Ch. 57:01. Third Schedule.*

**9.** The Stamp Duty Act shall not apply to the transfer and vesting of the real estate undertakings or the operational undertakings in the State or the Company, respectively, or to any subsequent lease of any part of the real estate undertakings which the State may grant to the Company, the Manager or any other corporate entity for so long as the State is or continues to be the owner of Caroni, the Company, the Manager or such other corporate entity.

**10.** (1) Notwithstanding the State Lands Act, the Manager shall be responsible for—

(a) the development of—

(i) the real estate undertakings prescribed in the Third Schedule;

(ii) the real estate undertakings for commercial and industrial use, as prescribed by Order of the President on the advice of the Minister with responsibility for town and country planning; and

(b) the management of the real estate undertakings identified in paragraph (a) subsequent to distribution.

(2) The President may by Order made in furtherance of paragraph (a)(ii) prescribe the real estate undertakings to be used for commercial and industrial purposes.

(3) The real estate undertaking vested in the State under this Act, that are not distributed for residential, commercial or industrial purposes shall be utilised in accordance with the State Lands Act or any other written law on the subject of land use.

(4) For the purposes of subsection (1)(a),

*Reports.*

“development” means the carrying out of building, engineering or such other operations, the provision of infrastructure or utilities on, in or under any land but does not include the making of any change of use of any buildings or land, or the subdivision of any land.

**11.** (1) The Manager shall submit in writing to the Minister, under the hand of the Chairman of its Board of Directors or its Chief Executive Officer, a report of all the development undertaken under section 10 in the form and manner determined by the Minister, within three months after the end of each year.

*Regulations. Non-application of certain Acts. Ch. 59:53, Ch. 59:54 Ch. 57:05.*

(2) The Minister shall lay the report in Parliament within three months from the date of its receipt from the Manager.

**12.** The Minister may make Regulations generally for the purpose of giving effect to this Act.

**13.** (1) Subject to subsection (2) from the appointed day, the Agricultural Small Holdings Tenure Act, the Land Tenants (Security of Tenure) Act and the State Land (Regularisation of Tenure) Act, shall not apply to the real estate undertakings vested in the State.

(2) This Act shall not operate to extinguish or otherwise derogate from any rights, benefits, legitimate expectation or real estate undertakings already acquired by or vested in any tenant or squatter under the Acts listed in subsection (1).
